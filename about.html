Our Mission

I DID NOT EVOLVE is a faith-based apologetics and education ministry dedicated to defending the full trustworthiness of Scripture. From the biblical account of creation to the global flood and the authority of Genesis, we provide clear, evidence-based answers to the toughest objections against biblical truth.

Scientific Integrity

We use biology, geology, and physics to demonstrate that life's complexity reveals an Intelligent Designer, not random processes.

Campus Awareness

We take the truth to university lecture halls through open-mic debates and Q&A sessions across Nigeria.

Future Leaders

We are raising a generation of young Nigerian apologists who can confidently answer any skeptic with grace and power.
