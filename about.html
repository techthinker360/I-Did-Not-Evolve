The Pursuit of Truth

We believe that life’s complexity and beauty aren't accidents of time, but the intentional design of the Creator.

Defending the Word

I DID NOT EVOLVE is a faith-based apologetics and education ministry dedicated to defending the full trustworthiness of Scripture—beginning with the biblical account of creation.

Using science, logic, and Scripture, we demonstrate that the complexity of life reveals an Intelligent Designer rather than random processes, while providing evidence-based answers to the toughest objections against biblical truth.

Our Foundations

Biblical Authority

We stand firmly on Genesis 1-11 as literal history. From the Global Flood to the historical Adam, we believe God's Word is the ultimate foundation for all knowledge.

Scientific Precision

True science always points back to the Creator. We explore DNA, Polonium Halos, and the Fossil Record to show that the evidence fits the Bible, not evolutionary myths.

Campus Mission

We believe the battle for the mind is happening in our universities. Our tour brings open-mic debates and resources directly to students across Nigeria.
