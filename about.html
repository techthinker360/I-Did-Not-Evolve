Our Mission

Defending the full trustworthiness of Scripture through science and logic.

I DID NOT EVOLVE is a faith-based apologetics and education ministry dedicated to defending the full trustworthiness of Scripture. From the biblical account of creation to the global flood, we provide clear, evidence-based answers to the toughest objections.

Scientific Integrity

We use biology, geology, and physics to demonstrate that life's complexity reveals an Intelligent Designer.

Campus Awareness

We take the truth to university lecture halls through open-mic debates and Q&A sessions across Nigeria.

Future Leaders

We are raising a generation of young Nigerian apologists who can confidently answer any skeptic with grace.
